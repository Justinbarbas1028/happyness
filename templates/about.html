Our Purpose

What We Do

HappYness Project Philippines is a charitable organization that began in December 2018 with the aim of improving the quality of life in local communities.

As a registered non-governmental organization recognized by the Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR) and the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC), we implement action plans for sustainable community improvement.

We provide assistance to underserved communities by supplying them with meals, education-related initiatives, groceries, shoes, slippers, school supplies, and other essentials across hospitals, fire protection stations, orphanages, public schools, elderly homes, and indigent communities.

Our Guiding Principles

Our Mission

The HappYness Project is dedicated to making helping easy by creating sustainable programs that nurture the heart, uplift lives, and build inclusive communities. Through our core programs — HappY Hearts, HappY Nanays, and HappY Kids — we champion acts of kindness that promote holistic well-being, social empowerment, and lasting change for those most in need.

  • Empower communities through sustainable initiatives
  • Foster inclusive and supportive environments
  • Drive positive change through collective action

Our Vision

A world where kindness is a way of life — empowering communities to grow in love, compassion, and shared happiness. We envision a Philippines where every individual has access to opportunities, support, and the resources they need to thrive.

  • Universal access to well-being and opportunity
  • Harmony between humanity and the environment
  • Legacy of hope for future generations
