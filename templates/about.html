WHAT WE DO

HappYness Project Philippines is a charitable organization that began in December 2018 with the aim of improving the quality of life in local communities. It is a non-governmental organization that has received recognition from the Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR) and the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC), allowing it to implement its action plan for community improvement.

The organization provides assistance to underserved communities by supplying them with meals, education-related initiatives, groceries, shoes, slippers, school supplies, and other essentials. HappYness Project Philippines has conducted close to 1,000 outreach activities, offering support to various institutions such as hospitals, fire protection stations, orphanages, public schools, elderly homes, and 80+ indigent communities all over the Philippines.

Our Mission & Vision

Guiding principles that drive our commitment to building happier, more sustainable communities worldwide.

Our Mission

The HappYness Project is dedicated to making helping easy by creating sustainable programs that nurture the heart, uplift lives, and build inclusive communities. Through our core programs — HappY Hearts, HappY Nanays, and HappY Kids — we champion acts of kindness that promote holistic well-being, social empowerment, and lasting change for those most in need.

  • Empower communities through sustainable initiatives
  • Foster inclusive and supportive environments
  • Drive positive change through collective action

Our Vision

A world where kindness is a way of life — empowering communities to grow in love, compassion, and shared happiness.

  • Universal access to well-being and opportunity
  • Harmony between humanity and the environment
  • Legacy of hope for future generations
