Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about HappyNess

What is HappyNess?

HappyNess is a community-focused platform that connects volunteers with meaningful opportunities. Track your impact, join communities, and make a difference in your local area.

How do I log my volunteer hours?

Once logged in, navigate to the Home page and click "Log Volunteer Hours" in the Quick Actions section. You can enter the activity details, duration, and the community it's associated with.

Can I join multiple communities?

Yes! You can join as many communities as you like. Each community tracks your contributions separately, and you can view your total impact across all communities on your profile.

How do I reset my password?

Click "Forgot password?" on the login page. Enter your email address and we'll send you a link to reset your password. Check your spam folder if you don't see it within a few minutes.

Is my data secure?

Absolutely. We use industry-standard security practices including encrypted passwords, CSRF protection, and secure sessions. Your personal information is never shared without your consent.

How do I switch between light and dark mode?

Click the sun/moon icon in the bottom-right corner of any page. Your preference is saved automatically and will persist across sessions.
